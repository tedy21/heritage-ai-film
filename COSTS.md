**Reading it:** narration errors are the F13 quota 429s and F15 experiments; every one was retried or covered by a fallback model. GPU time is dominated by Wan 2.2: **122.7 GPU min for 5 clips of 4 s** (≈ 24.5 min per clip with sequential offload, F19), against 0.5 min for the ACE-Step score. The 8 clip errors are the out-of-memory attempts (F19); their median drags the "median latency" column down to 15.4 s, while a successful clip took 1458–1486 s. That makes motion the most expensive part of the film by far, and the reason only 5 of 9 shots were rendered (4 used: the s03 clip was dropped for an invented person, F20).
